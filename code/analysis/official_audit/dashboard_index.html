Method and caveats

  • Elector counts come from Wikipedia constituency pages, which cite ECI results. Spot checks match published figures, but check against ECI statistical reports before quoting.
  • Electors and Census population are different measures. The voter-roll charts show drift on real rolls; the comparison with our maps uses Census 2011, since that is what the optimizer balances.
  • Our maps are balanced on 2011 population. They would drift too by 2024, so the fair claim is that they fix the 2011 imbalance, not today's.
  • In Nashik, five official seats come out non-contiguous on our units, which inflates the official taluka count there.
  • Taluka splits count talukas cut at all. Extra cuts count pieces beyond the minimum a taluka's population forces.
