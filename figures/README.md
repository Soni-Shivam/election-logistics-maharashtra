## 5. Transporting polling parties and EVMs
Is routing needed? Yes, for cost and for the dispatch centre, but not because of the clock:

- **Kilometres:** routing shared trips cuts vehicle-km by 77%, and choosing the dispatch centre saves another 14% (`05b`).
- **Deadline:** the 18:00 deadline is slack. Routes take about an hour, so the fleet is set by seats on the bus, not by time (`05c`).
- **Polling night:** the real time pressure is the receipt centre, where 40–51% of parties arrive in the busiest hour (`05d`).
