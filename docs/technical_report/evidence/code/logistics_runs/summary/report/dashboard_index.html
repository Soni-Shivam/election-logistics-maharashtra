Method and caveats

  • Demand: Census 2011 villages scaled to each seat's 2024 electors; people placed on WorldPop's 100 m grid with a settlement mask, so the walk inside a village counts.
  • Stations and dispatch centre: one Gurobi model per seat, solved within 0.5% of optimal. Transport: OR-Tools routing of buses (6 parties) and jeeps, all parties in place by 18:00.
  • Polling day: Monte Carlo queue simulation, 30 runs, arrivals following the 2024 hourly turnout; everyone in line at 18:00 votes.
  • City seats in Pune and Nashik are rebuilt approximately, so seat-level counts there are less reliable than district totals.
  • Costs are planning estimates: ₹25,000 per station, ₹5,000 per premises, ₹50 per voter-hour, bus and jeep hire plus per-km rates.
