Sangli Delimitation Lab

Eight Assembly constituencies for Sangli, drawn by a ReCom optimizer on 756 villages and city wards, then scored on real OpenStreetMap road distances and an exact polling-booth model. Every plan is compared against the official 2008 constituencies on the same 2011 Census population.

Booth model

An exact integer programme per seat (CBC, every solve proven optimal): each booth serves at most 1,500 electors (assumed 70% of 2011 population), nobody walks further than the limit by road, and cost = booths + voter travel + EVM transport from the seat's road-best depot. The shaded band is the range across all plans. It is barely visible because the boundaries hardly change the booth plan.

Search robustness

Each road-run profile was searched twice from different random seeds (2,000 steps × 4 restarts each). Close scores across seeds mean the result is not luck. The map explorer shows the better seed under each profile's own weights.

How it was computed

  1. prepare_sangli_inputs.py: Census 2011 PCA and SHRUG village polygons become 756 units (734 villages and towns, 20 approximate city wards, 2 empty land patches). Total population is 2,822,143.
  2. build_road_matrix.py: drivable OpenStreetMap roads (Geofabrik western zone) give shortest road distances between all 751 populated units.
  3. sangli_recom.py --distance-matrix: ReCom with simulated annealing. Every seat must be contiguous, within ±10% of 352,768, and free of narrow necks. Each seat's centre is its road 1-median, which works as the EVM depot.
  4. official_ac_map.py: the 2008 constituencies, rebuilt on the same units from SHRUG's village-to-constituency key.
  5. booth_mip.py: exact booth placement per seat on road distances at 2, 3 and 5 km walking limits.

Limits of these results

Population, not electors

Seats are balanced on 2011 Census population. The official seats were drawn on 2001 population, so part of their imbalance is twenty years of growth around the city.

Approximate city wards

Sangli-Miraj-Kupwad is split into 20 schematic wards. The official Miraj/Sangli line through the city is approximated, but those two seats exceed the band under any split, since together they hold more people than two seats may.

One point per village

Each village is a single point, so travel inside a village counts as zero. Booth and voter-distance figures measure travel between villages only.

Assumed costs

The elector ratio (0.70), booth cost and per-km costs are placeholders. The booth count depends mainly on the walking limit, not on these values.

Heuristic search

ReCom finds good plans, not proven-best ones. Rule checks are exact; scores are not certificates of optimality.

Not a delimitation

SC/ST reservation, Lok Sabha nesting and public consultation are not modelled. This is a research comparison.
